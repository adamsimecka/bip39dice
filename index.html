How dice make a Bitcoin seed word

A Bitcoin seed is a list of secret words. Each word comes from rolling dice. Try it once — takes a few seconds.

1. Make one word

Click the button. We roll 11 dice and turn them into one word from the official Bitcoin word list.

Your practice word

—

  1. Odd numbers → 0 · Even numbers → 1
  2. Those bits make a number from 0 to 2047
  3. That number picks a word on the BIP-39 list

2. A full seed needs 12 words

  1. Words 1–11 — roll dice like above (for real: use paper and physical dice).
  2. Word 12 — calculated from the first 11 (a checksum). Offline tools list the valid choices.

That’s it. 12 secret words = your wallet. Lose them and the bitcoin is gone. Share them and someone can steal it.

Demo only. Do not put money on this seed.

    Want 24 words instead?

    Same idea, but 23 dice-words + 1 checksum word (256-bit security). Most beginners are fine with 12.

    3. For real money — go offline

    Never trust a website (including this one) with a seed you’ll fund. Use paper, real dice, and an offline computer.

    • Print the offline guide (PDF)
    • Roll physical dice for words 1–11
    • Run the offline script for word 12
    • Write the 12 words on metal or paper — never a photo
