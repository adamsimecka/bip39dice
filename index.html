Seed length

Default is 12 words (128-bit entropy). Switch anytime before funding a practice seed — resets the builder.

Why generate a seed with dice?

A hardware wallet’s random-number generator is a black box. Dice let you watch every bit of entropy get created — physics, not firmware.

The trust problem

When a device creates your seed, you trust that its entropy is truly random, not backdoored, and not weakened by a firmware bug. The Mk3 advisory is a live reminder: device-generated seeds can become a single point of failure you cannot audit after the fact.

What dice buy you

Physical dice (ideally casino-grade) produce randomness you can observe. Convert rolls → bits → BIP-39 words with a method you can recompute offline — the same approach documented in veebch/Bip39-Dice.

How a dice seed becomes 12 words

Each BIP-39 word encodes 11 bits. For a 12-word seed you need 128 bits of entropy plus a 4-bit checksum baked into the last word.

The paper calculator (veebch method)

From the BIP39DiceManualCalculator.pdf in veebch/Bip39-Dice: for each of the first 11 words, roll 11 dice and map:

  • Odd face (1, 3, 5) → bit 0
  • Even face (2, 4, 6) → bit 1

Multiply each bit by powers of two (LSB first): 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 and sum them. That sum is the word index on the official list (0 = abandon … 2047 = zoo).

The 12th word is special: only 128 of the 2048 words produce a valid checksum. Offline, run 24thword.py (accepts 11 or 23 words), then pick one candidate with more dice.

12 vs 24: 12 words → 128-bit entropy, 128 last-word candidates, and a 4-bit checksum. 24 words → 256-bit entropy, 8 last-word candidates, and an 8-bit checksum. Both are valid BIP-39; many people start with 12.

Do it for real (offline checklist)

Use this site to learn. Use this checklist when real sats are on the line. Tick items as you prepare — state stays in this browser only.

Never enter a funded seed or real dice rolls into this website. Real generation happens on paper + air-gapped hardware only.

  • Private room, no cameras

    Phones out of the room. Cover laptop cameras. No smart speakers.

  • Fair dice + pen + paper

    Casino-grade d6 recommended. Print veebch’s BIP39DiceManualCalculator.pdf and the BIP-39 English wordlist before you go offline.

  • Generate 11 words on paper

    For each word: roll 11 dice → odd/even bits → sum powers of 2 → look up the word. Double-check every index.

  • Compute the last word offline

    On an air-gapped machine, clone veebch/Bip39-Dice and run python3 24thword.py (works for 11- or 23-word prefixes). You’ll get 128 candidates — pick one with dice.

  • Verify restore before funding

    Import all 12 words into an offline wallet, confirm addresses, wipe, restore again from the metal/paper backup, then send a small test amount.

  • Metal backup + destroy notes

    Stamp words into steel/titanium. Burn working paper (dice sequences, intermediate sums). Never photograph the seed.

  • If migrating from Mk3

    Read Coinkite’s full migration guidance. Prefer generating the new seed on an unaffected device (Mk4 / Q / Mk5). A strong BIP-39 passphrase is an interim option only — not a substitute for a clean seed when you can migrate.
